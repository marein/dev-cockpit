dev-cockpit extension

Sends what you pick, the console and drawings of a page to an assistant or a running coder. It needs the cockpit's address and a token from Settings › Browser in the cockpit.

Paste the token the cockpit showed you once.
Picking and drawing work in every tab without a click on the extension's button, and picking goes on through reloads and other pages. A small script in every page catches its console from its first script on. The picker and the drawing enter a page only while you use them. The browser's settings of the extension withdraw the access.
Safari also asks per website, through the extension's button: allow it on every website for picking and the console everywhere. Safari › Settings › Websites changes it later.

Use

Click the extension's button in the toolbar to pick elements in the tab. The panel beside the page holds the tray, the drawing and the console.

Click the extension's button in the toolbar to open the panel over the page with the tab's tray, drawing and console. Pick there picks elements. A click into the page closes the panel, the tray stays.

The browser asks once for access to the cockpit's address and, when ticked, every page. The extension calls no other address.
